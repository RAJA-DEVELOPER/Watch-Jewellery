Our Story

Four decades of passion for the extraordinary — where fine watchmaking meets the art of jewellery.

Est. 1987

A Legacy
Built on
Passion

Maison Lumière was founded in 1987 by Sir Edmund Ashworth, a former horologist with Audemars Piguet, and his wife Lady Caroline Ashworth, a jewellery designer trained at the Gemological Institute of America.

Their vision was singular: to create a space where the world's finest timepieces and jewellery could be experienced, not merely purchased. A sanctuary of excellence in the heart of Mayfair.

Visit Our Showroom

1987

The Foundation

Maison Lumière opens its doors at 47 Bond Street, London, with an inaugural collection of 12 Swiss timepieces and bespoke jewellery.

1995

First International Partnership

Patek Philippe selects Maison Lumière as an authorised partner — the first independent London boutique to receive this honour in a decade.

2003

The Dubai Expansion

A second showroom opens in Dubai's DIFC, bringing our curation to the Gulf's most discerning collectors.

2015

High Jewellery Atelier Launch

We launch our bespoke jewellery studio — creating one-of-a-kind commissions for clients worldwide.

2024

Celebrating 37 Years

Named 'Jewellery Retailer of the Year' by the UK Luxury Guild for the fifth consecutive year, serving over 12,000 global clients.

Our Philosophy

Crafted With
Intention

Every aspect of the Maison Lumière experience is guided by our three founding pillars of excellence.

Authenticity

Every piece in our collection is accompanied by full provenance documentation, serial verification, and manufacturer certification. We categorically refuse to stock any piece that cannot be authenticated to the highest standard.

Timelessness

We curate not for trends but for eternity. Our selection criteria demands that every piece holds enduring relevance — both as an object of beauty and as an investment that appreciates with time.

Service

From first enquiry to lifetime after-care, our dedicated client advisors provide a seamless, unhurried, and deeply personal experience — because luxury is not a product, it is a relationship.
