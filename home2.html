Our Heritage

Four Decades of
Uncompromising Excellence

Since 1987, Maison Lumière has been the trusted name for discerning collectors and connoisseurs. From our Bond Street atelier, we have curated relationships with over 40 of the world's most prestigious watch and jewellery maisons.

Every piece we present carries with it a story of extraordinary craftsmanship — and our promise of authenticity, backed by full provenance documentation and lifetime after-sales care.
