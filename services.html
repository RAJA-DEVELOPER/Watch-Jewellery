Watch Repair
Masterclass

Every watch repair at Maison Lumière is undertaken by watchmakers trained at the Ecole d'Horlogerie de Genève. We handle everything from routine service to complex movement overhaul, including tourbillons, chronographs, and grande complications.

  • Full movement disassembly, clean, and reassembly
  • Genuine manufacturer-spec replacement parts only
  • Water resistance testing and timing accuracy verification
  • Case and bracelet polishing to original finish
  • 2-year service warranty on all completed repairs

A standard service for a mechanical watch typically takes 3–6 weeks. Complex movements — tourbillons, perpetual calendars, and rattrapantes — may require 6–10 weeks. All timelines are quoted at the time of assessment, and we keep you updated throughout the process.
We are authorised service centres for Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, and IWC Schaffhausen. We also service most major Swiss brands as independent specialists, including Rolex, Omega, Jaeger-LeCoultre, Panerai, and Cartier. Please contact us for confirmation regarding your specific timepiece.
Our valuation reports include a full description of materials and construction, gemstone grading (4Cs for diamonds), current replacement value for insurance, estimated resale/estate value, dated authentication photos, and the signature of a GIA-certified gemologist. Reports are accepted by all major UK and international insurers.
